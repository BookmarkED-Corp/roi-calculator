OnShelf doesn't replace book-order work — librarians still curate. It compresses the overhead around the work so their hours go to judgment calls that actually require a librarian. The time modeled above is returned to the district to repurpose toward instruction.
Methodology & assumptions
Estimated cost(Hours per order × orders per year) × librarians × hourly rate. Labor is treated as a loaded rate (salary + benefits). Hours capture the full cycle per order — selection through approval.
OnShelf price$3 per student per year — canonical Bookmarked pricing. This is the subscription line item, not a budget estimate. Applied to enrolled student count.
Net to district(Labor saved % × estimated cost) minus OnShelf price. Represents dollars freed from overhead — available to repurpose toward instruction, staffing, or materials.
Full-time equivalentsTime returned (saved% × total hours) divided by 2,080 — the standard 52-week, 40-hour work year. Represents the equivalent staff capacity freed, not a headcount reduction.
Labor saved rangeDefault is 70% — the primary scenario in the Garland ISD cost-vs-price brief. Drag the slider to stress-test the case. At 30%, Garland's upper-end model still nets +$97,560/yr.
